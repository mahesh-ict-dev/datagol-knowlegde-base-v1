title: About AI generated dashboards
---

The Generate Dashboard feature enables users to quickly create insightful dashboards tailored to their specific roles or needs. You can either select from predefined personas or define your own custom dashboard through natural language descriptions.

Located under the **Actions** tab in the workbook, the **Generate Dashboard** option leverages AI to automatically identify potential insights within the selected workbook's data.

By choosing a specific persona such as Data Analyst or Business Executive, the AI formulates relevant questions and generates 8 to 10 charts representing different perspectives of the data. This process takes approximately the same amount of time as generating a single chart through the chart agent.

The AI dashboard generation is a fast and user-friendly way to visualize key information without requiring technical expertise.
