DataGOL offers a flexible way to visualize your data through configurable widgets. 

**Configuring a widget** involves defining what data is displayed on a widget and how it is presented visually. This involves the following:
-   **Selecting the specific columns for the x and y axis**
-   **Deciding if the y-axis should display cumulative values**
-   **Choosing from a range of technical indicators to overlay** 
-   **Fine-tuning the aesthetic details like numeric notations and chart label colors during configuration**

Whereas **setting the widget** focuses on the overall structure and orientation of the chart. This is where you decide:
-   **If multiple series should be stacked**
-   **Whether to display the x and y axes labels**
-   **If the chart should be rendered horizontally or vertically**

:::tip
-   **Configuration** deals with the data content and its immediate visual attributes.
-   **Settings** govern the broader layout and presentation style of the widget.
:::
