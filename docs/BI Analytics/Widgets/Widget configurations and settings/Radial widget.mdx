## Radial Chart

The Radial Chart displays data values as concentric arcs from a central point, ideal for comparing 3–10 categories on a single metric in a compact, visual format.

**When to use:** Comparing categories side by side or showing periodic performance where a bar chart feels too flat.

**How to create:**
1. Open the Visualizer and select your dimension and metric columns.
2. Choose **Radial** from the Visualization panel.
3. Configure categories, values, and Slice with Color from the Configuration panel on the right.

> **Tip:** Radial charts work best with fewer than 10 categories. For larger datasets, consider a bar chart or treemap instead.
