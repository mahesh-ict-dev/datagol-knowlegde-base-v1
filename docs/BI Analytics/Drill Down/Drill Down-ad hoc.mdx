### What is Ad-Hoc Drill-Down?
Ad-hoc drill-down lets you instantly explore your data without any pre-configuration. With just a right-click on any cell or column in a table or pivot, you can dive deeper into related dimensions—making it easy to answer follow-up questions on the fly.


### How to Use Ad-Hoc Drill Down ?
1. **Available On:**
   Any visualization.

2. **Steps:**  
   - Right-click a column value (for example, `Department = Sales`).
   - Select a drill-down option from the menu.
   - Choose the next dimension you want to explore (such as Gender, Location, or Manager).
   - Instantly view the data broken down by your selected dimension.

### When to Use Ad-Hoc Drill-Down ?
Use Ad-Hoc Drill-Down when you need quick, flexible, and on-the-spot exploration of your data without any predefined setup. It's perfect for scenarios where:

- You want to investigate unexpected changes or anomalies in your data.

- You need to explore relationships across any columns or dimensions on demand.

- You're working in a fast-paced environment (e.g., meetings, reviews) and want instant answers.

- You’re an analyst or power user who prefers dynamic, curiosity-driven exploration over fixed drill paths.
