After you have [created a visualization or widget](</docs/BI Analytics/Visualizer/Creating visualization or widget.mdx>), you can pin it to the dashboards. 
Pinning a widget to a dashboard allows you to keep important visualizations and key metrics in a central, easily accessible location. This provides a quick overview of your data and helps you monitor performance at a glance without having to navigate to different reports or visualizations. It centralizes information for better monitoring and decision-making.

To pin a visualization to a dashboard, do the following:

1. From the upper right corner of the generated widget, click the **Save** button.
2. Select the **Pin to Dashboard** option.
3. Specify the following details:
    -   Title 
    -   Description 
    -   Select a dashboard page to pin the widget
4.  Click the **Pin Visualization** button. The visualization is successfully pinned to the dashboard. Click the **Go to Dashboard** button to directly go to the dashboard page and view the pinned visualization.
