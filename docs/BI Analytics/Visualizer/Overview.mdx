The **Visualizer** is a crucial component for performing **Business Intelligence (BI) Analytics**. It's where you actively engage in **data visualization** to transform raw data into actionable insights, ultimately driving data-driven decision-making.

Within the Visualizer, you construct the widgets and tables that display your data.  You can customize various aspects such as:

* [**Widget types**](</docs/BI Analytics/Widgets/Widget Types.mdx>): Choose the most effective visual representation for your data (e.g., bar charts for comparisons, line graphs for trends over time and pie charts for proportions).
* [**Configurations**](</docs/BI Analytics/Widgets/Widget Configuration and Settings.mdx>): Adjust settings for each widget to optimize its clarity and highlight key metrics.
* [**Filters**](</docs/BI Analytics/Widgets/Widget Filters.mdx>): Refine the data shown in your widgets to focus on specific segments or periods, allowing for more targeted analysis.
* [**Drill-down paths**](</docs/BI Analytics/Drill Down/Drill Down.mdx>): Define interactive paths that enable users to explore data in more detail, moving from a high-level overview to granular specifics, which is a core capability of effective BI.

The data fueling these visualizations originates from **workbooks**. These workbooks are meticulously prepared, drawing data from your connected **data sources** and undergoing processes of **curation and transformation** through **pipelines** and **orchestrations**. In essence, the workbooks serve as the golden layer for  data modelling upon which the Visualizer builds its powerful BI insights.

Once these insightful widgets are created, they can be saved onto **dashboards**. Dashboards act as centralized, interactive hubs where various visualizations are brought together to provide a comprehensive, at-a-glance view of key business metrics, enabling users to quickly grasp performance, identify trends, spot anomalies, and make informed decisions based on the underlying BI analytics.
