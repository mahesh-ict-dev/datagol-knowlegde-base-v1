:::info
Before you log in, make sure your email address has been **verified**.
:::


1.  Go to the DataGOL [sign-in page](https://app.datagol.ai/login?redirect=/home) and use your **verified email address** and the **password you configured** to log in.
 
2.  Alternatively, you can select **Login with Google** and use your company email address. 
    ![Login-with-google](/img/att_131_for_220692503.png)
    Your default role upon creation is **User**. Account administrators can assign different roles. 
    To update your personal information or reset your password, navigate to the **User** section, which is found under **Accounts**.

    :::warning
    **Important:** DataGOL recommends **not using Gmail addresses** for login with Google, as this feature will be discontinued for Gmail accounts in the future.
    :::
