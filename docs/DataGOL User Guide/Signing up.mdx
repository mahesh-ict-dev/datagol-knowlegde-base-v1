To sign up for DataGOL, you will start by navigating to the [sign-in page](https://app.datagol.ai/login?redirect=/home). As a new user, you will need to create an account, which involves configuring your login credentials. After successfully setting up your account, DataGOL will send you a welcome email that you must verify to activate your account. Once verified, you can then log in using the email and password you configured.

To create an account, follow these instructions:

1. Go to the DataGOL [sign-in page](https://app.datagol.ai/login?redirect=/home) and click the **Create an Account** option.  
   <ImagePopup src="/img/Login/Login-1.png" alt="Create Account Option" caption="Create Account Option" />

2. In the **Welcome** dialog box, enter a valid email ID and click **Continue**. Providing a valid email ID is mandatory.  
   <ImagePopup src="/img/Login/login2.png" alt="welcome-dialog-box" caption="welcome-dialog-box" />

3. In the **Create your account** dialog box, specify the following details:  
   <ImagePopup src="/img/Login/login3.png" alt="create-your-account" caption="create-your-account" />
     
   - **First name**  
   - **Last name**  
   - **Password**  
   - **Confirm Password** 
   
4. Click **Submit** button. A verification email is sent from DataGOL `<no-reply@datagol.ai>`.
    <ImagePopup src="/img/att_11_for_220692503.png" alt="New-signup-confirmation" caption="New-signup-confirmation" />  

4. Verify the email account. After email verification, you can [log in](logging-in-to-datagol) using the email address and password configured.

:::tip [NOTE]

The **first user** to create an account with a new domain becomes the **account admin** by default.

:::
