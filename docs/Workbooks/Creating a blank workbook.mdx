To create a blank workbook, do the following:

On the **Home** page, from the left navigation panel, click **Workspace**, and from the list of workspaces, open a workspace.

From the left panel, at the bottom, click **Create a Blank Workbook**. A blank worksheet is created. You can add the desired columns and furnish the blank workbook with information.
