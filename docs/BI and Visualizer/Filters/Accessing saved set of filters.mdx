1.  From a workspace, access a dashboard.
    
2.  On the dashboard, from the upper right corner, click the filter settings icon.
    
    ![accessing-the-saved-set-of-page-level-or-dashboard-level-filters1](/img/visualizer/image-20250514-102809.png) 
    
3.  In the **Page Filter Settings** box, click the **Saved Filters** tab. The saved filters are listed here.
    
   ![accessing-the-saved-set-of-page-level-or-dashboard-level-filters1](/img/visualizer/image-20250514-105603.png)
