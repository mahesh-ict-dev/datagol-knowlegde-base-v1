Within the dashboard interface, you possess the ability to define specific views suited to your analytical needs by applying filters. These customized filter configurations can be persistently stored for your convenience, allowing for their subsequent and immediate retrieval and application. This feature empowers you to create and save preferred data perspectives, ensuring efficient access to the insights most relevant to your work instantly.

To save a set of page level/dashboard level filters, do the following:

1.  From a workspace, access a dashboard.
    
2.  On the dashboard, from the upper right corner, click the **Settings** icon, choose **Manage filters** and [add page level/dashboard level filters](https://saasxl.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/documentation/pages/392527873/Data+visualization+guide#Adding-page-level%2Fdashboard-level-filters "https://saasxl.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/documentation/pages/392527873/Data+visualization+guide#Adding-page-level%2Fdashboard-level-filters").
    
3.  From the upper right corner, click the filter settings icon.
    
    ![saving-set-of-page-level-or-dashboard-level-filters1](/img/visualizer/image-20250514-102809.png)
    
4.  In the **Page Filter Settings** box, in the **Current** filters tab, check the filters and click **Save**.
    
    ![saving-set-of-page-level-or-dashboard-level-filters1](/img/visualizer/image-20250514-104931.png)
    
5.  Add a title and description for the filters and click **Confirm**.
    
     ![saving-set-of-page-level-or-dashboard-level-filters1](/img/visualizer/image-20250514-105047.png) 
    
    These filters are listed in the **Saved filters** tab.
