Filters are essential components in Business Intelligence (BI) analytics, both within individual widgets and across entire dashboards. They allow users to narrow down large datasets and focus on specific, relevant information, making data analysis more efficient and insights clearer.  Filters are applied at different levels to control the scope of data displayed. In DataGOL, the following types of filters can be applied for a visualization:

**Dashboard filters or page-level filters** are the broadest type of filters. They apply to all widgets across all pages within an entire dashboard. When you apply a dashboard filter, every chart, table, and data point in the entire report will update to reflect that filter condition.

**Widget-level filters** are granular filters, which allows you to define specific criteria that will narrow down the data displayed only within that particular visual, without affecting any other visuals on the same page or in the entire report.
