You can change the layout of your dashboards by changing the order of the widgets in the Dashboard.

1.  From a workspace, access a dashboard.
    
2.  On the dashboard, from the upper right corner, click the **Settings** icon and then choose **Manage Layouts & Widgets.**
    
    ![switch-workbooks-in-a-dashboard](/img/visualizer/image-20250514-144000.png)
    
3.  Drag and re-order the widgets as required.
    
4.  From the upper right corner, click **Done** button.
