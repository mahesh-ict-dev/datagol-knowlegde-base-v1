**Date released: April 22, 2026**


## New Features and Enhancements

### AI

- **Custom Agent – Conversation Grouping:** Improved the Agents side panel by grouping conversations into collapsible time-based sections and also by agent type, making it easier to navigate and manage chat history.
<ImagePopup src="/img/release_notes/2026/2026/April/chat_grouping.png" alt="Chat Grouping" caption="Chat Grouping" />

### Workspaces and Workbooks

- **Workbook/Workspace Delete UX:** Now we have delete protection enabled for workbook and workspace deletion to avoid accidental deletions.
<ImagePopup src="/img/release_notes/2026/2026/April/delete_ux.png" alt="Delete UX" caption="Delete UX" />

- **Workbook Append/Import UX:** When a user removes a new/unmapped field, they have a clear option to undo the action.
