## Enhancements
### AI
- [Audio & Video Files Now Supported in Workspace Documents](/docs/AI%20Agents/rag-agent#5-audio--video-files-now-supported-in-workspace-documents) You can now upload .mp3 and .mp4 files to Workspace Documents and query their contents just like any other document. Audio files are transcribed automatically, and video files have their audio extracted and transcribed — all using the Whisper speech-to-text model. The resulting transcripts are chunked, embedded, and indexed for semantic search, so you can ask natural language questions against meetings, recordings, and any spoken content in your workspace.
