**Date released: June 22, 2026**


## Enhancements
### Platform
- **Lakehouse Access Control for New Users:** Admins can now configure whether newly created users automatically get access to the Lakehouse layer. When restricted, the Lakehouse section is hidden from the user's view and the "Add Datasource" step is removed from the onboarding flow — keeping new users focused on the right experience from day one. The setting is available as a company-level toggle in Admin Platform configuration and applies to users created through both workspace invitations and direct registration.
- **Gemini Models Now Available via OpenRouter:** Gemini models are now accessible through OpenRouter, expanding the selection of AI models available for your agents and company configuration.
- **Improved AI Request Traceability:** Message IDs are now included in LiteLLM request tags and context, making it easier to trace, debug, and audit AI model requests across your environment.
