## Enhancements
### AI

- **More Reliable Long-Running Agent Tasks:** Long-running agent tasks no longer drop connections mid-execution. Heartbeat responses are now sent during extended operations, keeping the connection alive and ensuring results are delivered reliably even when tasks take significant time to complete.
- **Under the Hood: AI Service Improvements:** This release includes internal platform improvements that make the AI service faster to maintain and more consistent across all agent types.
