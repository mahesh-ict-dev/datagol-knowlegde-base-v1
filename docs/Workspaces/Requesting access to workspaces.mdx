To request access to a workspace, do the following:

1.  On the **Home** page, from the left navigation panel, click **Workspace**. This displays the **Workspace** page, listing all workspaces.
2.  From the upper right corner, click the **Join** button.
3.  In the **Join a Workspace** dialog box, you'll see workspaces that require access requests.
4.  Click **Request Access** for the workspace you wish to join.

    ![Request Access button](/img/image-20250512-092534.png)

    Your pending approvals are listed in the **Pending** tab.

    ![Pending tab](/img/image-20250515-103528.png)
