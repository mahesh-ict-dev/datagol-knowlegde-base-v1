# Connect Google Spaces
 
Index messages and conversations from Google Chat spaces.
 
## Steps
 
### 1. Authorize access *(Required)*
- Enter a **Connection name** (e.g. "Google Spaces Connector")
- Click **Connect with Google Spaces** to authenticate via OAuth
### 2. Initial Sync
- Optionally check **Start syncing right after connecting** to begin indexing immediately upon setup
### 3. Save
- Click **Save connector** to complete setup, or **Cancel** to discard
