From the Manager Filters section, you can rearrange the filters. Based upon on the rearrangement the hierarchy of the dependent filters changes

1.  From a workspace, access a dashboard.
    
2.  On the dashboard, from the upper right corner, click the **Settings** icon and then choose **Manage Filters.** In the **Manage Filters** box, all the filters are listed.
    
3.  Click the **Reorder Filters** button from the upper right corner. Once activated, you can intuitively reorder the filters in the list by dragging and dropping them into your desired sequence.
