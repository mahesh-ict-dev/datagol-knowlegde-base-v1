You can add users to an orchestration. If they do not already have access to associated pipelines, they can be granted permissions. Only Lakehouse members can be added, and they will receive creator permissions by default.

1.  On the DataGOL **Home** page, from the left navigation panel, click **Lakehouse** > **Orchestration**.
    
2.  In the **Orchestrations** page, click the link of an orchestration.
    
3.  In the **Summary** page, click the **Add** button.
    
    ![adding members to orchestration](/img/orchestration/image-20250511-164540.png)
    
4.  In the **Add members to Orchestration** box, select the users whom you want to add to the orchestration and click **Add**.
    
    ![adding members to orchestration](/img/orchestration/image-20250511-164731.png)
