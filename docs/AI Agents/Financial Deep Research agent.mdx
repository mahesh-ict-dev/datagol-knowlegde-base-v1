The Financial Deep Research Agent is a powerful **multi-agent architecture** designed to perform in-depth, comprehensive financial analysis on any organization. It operates by delegating complex research tasks to a team of specialized, independent AI agents, ensuring a thorough and data-driven report.

The agent system functions like a team of financial analysts, with each member focusing on a specific area.

-   **Multi-agent architecture:** The core is a system of multiple specialized agents, which may include:
    
    -   Cash and liquidity specialist: analyzes a company's short-term financial health and ability to meet immediate debts.
        
    -   Current liability specialist: focuses on short-term obligations and their impact.
        
    -   Revenue growth specialist: investigates sales performance, trends, and growth drivers.
        
    -   Gross profit specialist: examines profitability at the cost of goods sold level.
        
    -   Other agents: perform tasks like current asset analysis, debt structure analysis, and holistic risk assessment.
        
-   **Independent research:** all agents have independent access to the internet to gather up-to-date, relevant data, ensuring the analysis is grounded in the best possible research.
    
-   **Structured reporting:** it compiles the findings from all specialized agents into a **single, structured, and comprehensive report** with distinct sections, providing a holistic financial view.
    

###  FDR agent benefits

-   **Depth and rigor**: Receive a report that mimics the structured analysis of a professional financial analyst, covering all critical aspects from liquidity to long-term risk and growth.

-   **Time savings**: What would take a human analyst hours or days of research is completed in a matter of moments, automating the data gathering and initial synthesis.

-   **Comprehensive view**: The multi-agent system ensures **no critical financial area is overlooked**, as specialized agents simultaneously investigate cash, debt, revenue, profit, and risk.

-   **Actionable insights**: The report concludes with an **overall outlook and key items for the investor**, providing a clear summary and direction for decision-making.

-   **Transparency**: The process often shows the **tool calls and steps** the agent is taking (e.g., "analyzing cash and liquidity"), providing insight into its reasoning.


###  Using the FDR agent

Follow these simple steps to initiate a deep financial analysis:

#### **Step 1: Formulate the research query**

1.  Navigate to the agent's input interface.
    
2.  Clearly state your intent, the **company name**, and the **goal of the analysis**.
    
    -   **Tip:** frame your question to request a comprehensive financial report.
        
    -   **Example query:** _"I want to be an investor in [Company Name], which is a [Company Industry/Focus]. Help me with the financial analysis of this company."_
        

#### **Step 2: Observe agent initialization**

1.  After submitting your query, the agent will begin its process.
    
2.  Look for the system to display its internal working process, which includes:
    
    -   The generation of **internal "to-dos"** (the research plan).
        
    -   A sequence of **tool/agent calls** (e.g., "Invoking tool call: analyzing the cash and liquidity for [Company Name]").
        

#### **Step 3: Review the final financial summary**

1.  Wait for the process to complete, at which point the agent will compile and present the final summary report.
    
2.  Review the report, which is organized into clear, segmented sections:
    
    -   **Executive summary** (key findings upfront)
        
    -   **Cash and liquidity**
        
    -   **Current assets**
        
    -   **Current liability analysis**
        
    -   **Debt structure analysis**
        
    -   **Gross and profit margin**
        
    -   **Revenue analysis**
        
    -   **Risk assessment**
        
    -   **Overall outlook and key items for the investor**
