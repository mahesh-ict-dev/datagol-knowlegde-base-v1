# What is Drill-Down?

Drill-down is a powerful data exploration technique that lets you move from a high-level summary to more detailed views. In dashboards or reports, you can click on a data point (like a bar, metric, or chart segment) to reveal deeper layers of information. This helps you quickly investigate trends, spot anomalies, and understand performance breakdowns—all without leaving your dashboard.

**Why use drill-down?**
- Instantly explore the “why” behind your numbers
- Uncover hidden patterns or outliers
- Save time by navigating data hierarchies in a single view
