1.  On the DataGOL **Home** page, from the left navigation panel, click **Lakehouse** > **Orchestration**. The list of created orchestrations is displayed.
    
2.  Click the **Run** button corresponding to an orchestration. You can also open the orchestration link and click the **Run** button from the **Summary** tab.
