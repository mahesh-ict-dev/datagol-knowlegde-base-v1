To edit page level filters, do the following:

1.  From a workspace, access a dashboard.
    
2.  On the dashboard, from the upper right corner, click the **Settings** icon and then choose **Manage Filters.** In the **Manage Filters** box, all the filters are listed.
    
    <ImagePopup src="/img/visualizer/image-20250514-110151.png" alt="editing-page-level-filters1" caption="editing-page-level-filters1" />
    
3.  Click a filter and edit the details.
