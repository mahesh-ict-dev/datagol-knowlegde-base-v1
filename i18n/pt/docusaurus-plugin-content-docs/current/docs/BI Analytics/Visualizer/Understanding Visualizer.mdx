 Here is how the Visualizer works:
  <ImagePopup src="/img/visualizer.png" alt="Visualizer Home" caption="Visualizer Home" style={{ width: '50px' }} />


1. **Adding the Workbook:**  
   Start by selecting or adding a workbook to begin your analysis.  
   [How to Add a Workbook](/docs/BI%20Analytics/Visualizer/Select%20workbook)

2. **Auto-Populated Columns:**  
   Columns from the selected workbook are automatically displayed for you to choose from.

3. **Selecting Columns:**  
   Add columns to the search bar to include them in your visualization.  
   [Learn more about Selecting Columns](/docs/BI%20Analytics/Visualizer/Select%20Columns)

4. **Changing the Visualization Type:**  
   Easily switch between different visualization types (e.g., bar, pie, line) to best represent your data.  
   [Learn more about Visualization Types](/docs/BI%20Analytics/Widgets/Widget%20Types)

5. **Configuration Panel:**  
   Fine-tune your widget’s configuration.  
   [See Widget Types & Configuration](/docs/BI%20Analytics/Widgets/Widget%20Configuration%20and%20settings)

6. **Settings Panel:**  
   Modify the widget’s title, subtitle, and help message for better understanding.  
   [See Widget Types & Configuration](/docs/BI%20Analytics/Widgets/Widget%20Configuration%20and%20Settings)

7. **Drill Down:**  
   Configure drill-down layers to explore data in more detail.  
   [Learn about Drill Down](/docs/BI%20Analytics/Drill%20Down)

8. **Details Panel:**  
   View the SQL query behind your widget for transparency and troubleshooting.  
   [Learn about Details Panel](/docs/BI%20Analytics/Visualizer/Details%20Panel)

9. **Adding Formulas:**  
   Create custom formulas for advanced BI calculations.  
   [Understanding Formulas on BI](/docs/BI%20Analytics/Formulas%20&%20Parameters/BI%20Formula)

10. **Adding Parameters:**  
    Use parameters to make your BI platform more dynamic and interactive.  
    [Understanding Parameters on BI](/docs/BI%20Analytics/Formulas%20&%20Parameters/Parameters)

11. **Save Widget/Visualization:**  
    - **Pin to Dashboard:** Add your widget to a dashboard for easy access.  
    [Lear about pinning a visualization](/docs/BI%20Analytics/Visualizer/Save%20Widget/Pin%20to%20Dashboard)
    - **Save as Draft:** Save your work-in-progress for later editing.

        [Lear about creating drafts](/docs/BI%20Analytics/Visualizer/Save%20Widget/Save%20As%20Draft)
